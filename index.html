Meet the Instructor – Hao Zheng

Computer Science & AI Educator | Programming Instructor

With 10 years of programming experience, including 8 years in software development and algorithm engineering at a leading technology company, I have built a strong foundation in computer science and software development.

With 5 years of teaching experience, I enjoy working with young learners and helping them discover the joy of learning through coding. I understand that every child learns differently and that some concepts take time, patience, and the right approach to understand.

That's why I personally design and continuously refine my curriculum and teaching materials, keeping students' learning needs, interests, and developmental stages in mind. Through clear explanations, patient guidance, and hands-on projects, I aim to make learning computer science both approachable and enjoyable.

My goal is not simply to teach students how to code, but to help them become curious thinkers, confident problem-solvers, and creative learners who can turn their ideas into reality.
